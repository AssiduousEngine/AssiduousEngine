# Millbrook - Credits

Made with AssiduousEngine. Third-party assets, with thanks:

## Models

All by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/);
CC0 asks for no credit, and this is given gladly. `Tools/kenney.py` copies the ones Millbrook uses
out of the packs.

- **Mini Characters**: the villagers and the player, with the clips they share
  (`Licenses/Kenney-MiniCharacters-CC0.txt`).
- **Fantasy Town Kit**: the village (`Licenses/Kenney-FantasyTownKit-CC0.txt`).
- **Nature Kit**: grass, the stream, trees, bushes, flowers and stones (`Licenses/Kenney-NatureKit-CC0.txt`).

## Sky

- **Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)** by Greg Zaal (original) and Jarod Guest (sky edits),
  from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky),
  [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  (`Licenses/PolyHaven-Kloofendal48dPartlyCloudy-CC0.txt`). The default sky of a new project,
  unchanged.

## Fonts

- **Roboto** by Google, the UI font (cooked as a distance-field font, `Content/Fonts`), [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)
  (`Licenses/Roboto-NOTICE.txt`).

## Sounds and music

- The sounds (the cart, the splash, the knock, the cat, the chime) and the fair's tune are
  generated by `Tools/sounds.py`, the project's own.

Everything else (the scenes, the conversations, the sequences, the screens, the scripts and the
profiles) is the project's own.
