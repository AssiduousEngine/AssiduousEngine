# Millbrook

A village on the evening of its fair, told in conversations: the sample for the engine's dialogue
plugin, built through the engine's MCP tools from Kenney's CC0 kits.

## The game

You arrive in Millbrook as the fair is meant to open, and the village is nowhere near ready. The
mayor sends you round the square: the baker's stall has no sign, the miller's cart has lost a wheel,
and Pip has lost her cat. Walk up to anyone and press Interact to talk; what they say depends on
what you have done and whom you have met.

- **The baker's sign**: the guard at the gate has it in his keeping, and once you bring word the
  baker walks out and hangs it.
- **The miller's wheel**: it came off on the way up from the stream. He goes round the back of the
  mill with you to look for it, and once it is back on the cart rolls into the square.
- **Pip's cat**: she last saw it by the windmill. Go and look, then tell her.

After each, you tally what is left; when all three are done the mayor calls everyone to the
fountain, the lanterns are lit and the fair opens, then the credits. The village keeps what you have
done in its save, and a game saved partway through a conversation goes on from there.

Controls: W A S D (or the left stick) to walk, relative to the camera; Left Shift (pressing the left
stick) to run; E (A) to talk, to go on past a line and to choose the focused answer (the mouse
chooses one too); Q and R (the shoulders) to turn the view a quarter. On a touch screen: a stick on
the left half to walk, and buttons on the right to talk, run and turn.

## What it shows

- **Dialogue files** (`Tools/dialogue/*.dialogue`): conversations as text, with lines, choices,
  flags, `if` branches, jumps across files, and the staging a conversation does itself: a speaker's
  clip, a sound at them, a fade, a sequence it waits for.
- **The cast** (`Dialogue/Cast`): each speaker's shown name, moods, clips and the entity that plays
  them in the village.
- **The game's own commands**: `walk <who> to <where> [and wait]` and `errand <where>`, declared in
  the plugin's settings and carried out by the game's script; an errand leaves the conversation with
  its place kept, and the game resumes it.
- **Flags the game provides**: `fair_cat_seen`, set by the game when you reach the windmill.
- **The stock box**: the plugin's own dialogue box, restyled by the sample's sheet.
- **A cutscene with lines**: the ending is a sequence with a camera cut, an event that lights the
  lanterns, a sound, a fade and a Dialogue Lines track, each line held until the player goes on.
- **Saves**: flags and a conversation's place, resumed on the next start.

## Project layout

- `Project.xml`: the manifest: the village scene (`Scenes/Millbrook`), the game script
  (`Scripts/Millbrook`), the input map, the GUI sheet, and the dialogue plugin enabled with its
  settings (the cast and dialogue assets for new files, the commands, the provided flags, the stock
  box and its sheet).
- `Sources/`: the raw sources the assets import from: the dialogue files, the AngelScript scripts,
  the GUI markup and its sheets, the Kenney models and the generated sounds.
- `Content/`: the asset envelopes (`*.xasset`) and their sidecars: the village scene, the models,
  the cast, the dialogue files and their dialogue assets, the two sequences, the sounds, the dusk
  environment and post profile, and the screens.
- `Tools/`: the authoring scripts that build the game through the editor's MCP tools. Not game
  content; `Tools/README.md` says what each does, in order.
- `CREDITS.md` and `Licenses/`: the Kenney kits, the sky and the font.
- `Cooked/`, `.cache/`, `Editor/`, `Dist/`: generated, and gitignored; the tools rebuild them.

The models are Kenney's Mini Characters, Fantasy Town Kit and Nature Kit (CC0); the sounds and the
fair's tune are generated by `Tools/sounds.py`; the sky is Poly Haven's (CC0) and the UI font is
Roboto (Apache 2.0). All are credited in `CREDITS.md` with their licences in `Licenses/`.
