# Holdout - Credits

Made with AssiduousEngine. Third-party assets, with thanks:

## Models

- **Toon Shooter Game Kit** by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/toonshootergamekit.html),
  [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  (`Licenses/Quaternius-ToonShooterGameKit-CC0.txt`): the soldier, the hazmat suit and the enemy
  with their animations, the pistol, the SMG and the shotgun, and every piece of the yard and the
  town around it (fences, containers, sandbags, barriers, crates, the wrecked car, barrels, street
  lights, buildings and trees). Used unchanged.

## Sky

- **Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky)** by Greg Zaal (original) and Jarod Guest (sky edits),
  from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky),
  [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  (`Licenses/PolyHaven-Kloofendal48dPartlyCloudy-CC0.txt`). The default sky of a new project,
  unchanged.

## Sound effects

- **Kenney** ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl)), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  (`Licenses/Kenney-CC0.txt`): Impact Sounds (a shot landing, the player hurt, an enemy down, a
  reload), Interface Sounds (a weapon change, the pickups, a wave starting, the menus' click, the
  end of a run held or overrun) and Sci-Fi Sounds (a barrel's blast, the gunners' rounds). Renamed
  for what they do in the game; otherwise unmodified.

## Fonts

- **Roboto** by Google, the UI font (cooked as a distance-field font, `Content/Fonts`), [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0)
  (`Licenses/Roboto-NOTICE.txt`).

Everything else (the scene, the C#, the gunshots made by `Tools/sounds.py` and the profiles) is the
project's own.
