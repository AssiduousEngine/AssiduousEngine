## The sequencer, Q3 and Q4 (2026-11-21)

- **A view from another camera starts its history fresh** (acbb7a2e9): when a scene's camera pick
  moved to another camera, the view kept its history slot and blended the old camera's TAA,
  reflections, GI and exposure into the first frames. A history slot remembers the camera it was
  drawn from; another is a cut.
- **Camera cuts** (092662268): a track makes its bound camera the scene's view while a section
  runs, through claims on the view (runtime state, never saved): the play begun last wins, letting
  go hands the view back. Plays gained a start order for it.
- **Fades** (3f87bc20a): a colour mixed over the whole view, the tonemap's last step, so the HUD and
  subtitles draw over it; a claim on the scene's post-process system, won as the camera is.
- **Skeletal clips** (148ef0fb5): sections on an actor's pose overlay (PoseOverlay::DriveOneShot),
  so a seek lands on the frame while the graph underneath keeps running.
- **Sounds** (3f78d54e2): sections in the scene's audio group, spatial at the actor or flat; a voice
  starts part way into a section entered late and is moved back when it drifts, so it stays on the
  timeline through seeks and speed changes. Plays tell runners when they pause.
- **The editor** (b7bbbef1b, bcf87f202, dfe4246c5, 9de8c3cd8, 0779c2b79, bf6a68427, 3d5249e12): the
  sequence document with its own history and a restoring preview; section rows in the toolkit's
  dopesheet (bars moved and trimmed as one gesture); each track kind saying how its keys and
  sections are edited, so a kind a plugin adds edits with no list to extend; the dopesheet and
  curve view over the document; the kinds' fields in the inspector; the panel under the scene's
  viewport (Cast, Track, Section, markers and holds, its own undo) and a double click that adds a
  section.
- **MCP** (74f0019f6): `sequence_read`, `sequence_edit` (a list of edits written once, or nothing)
  and `sequence_preview` on the editor host.
- **A track's runner may hold the play** (bda7bfc82), for a line of dialogue on the timeline that
  waits to be read. A per-scene start order is in the backlog to revisit (074260977).
- The spec records Q3 and Q4 built (804550981, 247608c79), with what differs from the plan: sections
  are dopesheet rows, the panel is a pane beside the animation panel, preview cuts and the panel's
  sound on play are not built. The commits were rewritten to drop shorthand namespace aliases
  (`seq`, `propanim`), and the specs cite the new hashes (465f5b497).

game's text format kept and its game-specific statements declared as commands, a per-run runner, a
facade, a line track, a stock box, an editor page and MCP tools.

- **The package** (8255cc7c5): four parts in Code/Plugins/Dialogue, the first official plugin the
  engine's hosts link (players and templates, the editor, the tools); a project loads it once its
  plugins list enables it.
- **The format and the cook** (c574eb16b, 14553e913, 186c459d7, fc4cfdc24, 0e5b12608, e1e3d75d5,
  153549593, 6a4724117, aa50a12a7): compiled conversations and the cast; a parser that reports every
  problem at its line in one pass; a command notation shared by the built-in statements; the cook's
  checks (speakers, moods, clips, flags, jumps, ids, unreachable statements, `{Action}` against the
  input maps, entities against the conversation's level as warnings); assets named through the
  dialogue assets, never by path; each file naming its own cast and assets; ids unique within a
  file, a jump reaching another by name; and a file depending on the others' text, not their
  recipes, which ended a read-dependency cycle.
- **The editor around plugins** (1acc1b497, 150a4e36f, aee648ce7, e2a3182fe, 36e816436): a page

  9f43b20ec, 7fa062b24): a box on the screen stack that types lines and offers answers to the pad,

## Native behaviors, N0 to N2 (2026-11-21 to 2026-11-22)

`Specs/native-behaviors.md`, split from the script backends spec with its open questions decided
(805e7478d) and its seam mapped (f7238a93f): only fields marked `behaviorProperty` are properties,
native classes are found by reflection alone, SetProperty is on the object, and ScriptObject is
renamed BehaviorObject.

- **The seam** (0e8d9776e, dd67c7dd7, 1196911c2, a9133c625, e608d4e76): facades find the services
  of the call running now, a script's or native code's (ServiceScope); BehaviorObject and
  BehaviorMethod; properties written through SetProperty; the run host's VM half is
  ScriptBehaviorClasses; the run behavior is made through Instantiate as every behavior is.
- **Native classes** (448b286ea, 561191f44, c8dd9b8f1, abeb06376, ac14be117): a crash report names
  what the crashing thread was doing; RunBehavior, SceneBehavior and EntityBehavior as C++ bases,
  made by the run host with no VM, a fault disabling the behavior; the Native Behavior asset and its
  cook from the class's reflection; File > New's Native Behavior; NativeSample's Bobber, Showcase and
  NativeGame, and `native_behavior_list`.
- **Conformance** (320d3d6c6): one probe in AngelScript, Luau and C++ comes out the same.
  Found: an inactive entity only suspends its behaviors; onDisable and onEnable follow the
  behavior's own switch.
- **Docs** (7b7f1670b): behaviors in C++ in the scripting guide and the changelog.
- Lanes: clang, gcc and clang-shared 211/211 at 7b7f1670b.
- **The run behavior takes a native behavior's asset** (0566b3e44): the setting named the script
  asset type; it now names what both cook to, BehaviorClass, and SourceAssetTypesFor takes every
  builder of a cooked form, which also left Native Behavior assets out of the scene's behavior
  picker.
- **NativeSample's scene plays its native behaviors** (b2cd1713a): the row's cubes bob, the scene's
  behavior is Showcase and the run behavior NativeGame.
- **The seam's cost, measured and fixed** (063608187): against f7238a93f, Luau's calls into the
  engine were 7 to 11% slower, from a second thread_local slot reached through a second accessor in
  another library. One CallSlots thread_local found once per scope brings every measure back
  within noise. Lanes: clang, gcc and clang-shared 211/211.
- **A native behavior column in the scene measures** (d52869221): a native behavior's handler costs
  about 165 ns, near Luau's 200 against a component's 6; the dispatch path, which scripts pay too, is
  what to look at next. The spec's Risks hold the numbers.
- Owed before the spec is archived: the user's check of NativeSample's native behaviors in the
  editor.
